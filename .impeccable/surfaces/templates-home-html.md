# Surface brief: the whole site (home, publications, CV, news)

Scope: `templates/`, `assets/css/site.css`, `assets/js/`. Mode: Persuade on the home page; Read on publications, CV and news.

Audience and job: researchers who want the work, and people evaluating a profile who want trajectory and CV, weighted equally. Action: open a paper, the CV, or email. Proof: eight publications, the news log, the Best Paper Award, the portrait. Constraints: text is fixed (headline, "News" heading, no topic cards, no slogans); four pages and all URLs stay; content readable without JavaScript; dark mode; reduced motion.

Wrong even if polished: an academic template, a cold impersonal page, a creative-agency showpiece.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An AI-agents researcher's site as a flat-ink Italian modernist programme: a multi-agent system at work that the visitor can rearrange and extend (direction locked by the user on 2026-11-15, with the steer: agents, not mathematics). It refuses the category arrangement of portrait sidebar, bio and news list on cream paper.

OWN-WORLD: Pale dusk-sky paper and blue-black ink, graphite for secondary text, and four flat poster inks drawn from the portrait: wine (home), pine green (publications), cobalt (CV), sun yellow (news). Fields keep their ink in both themes and carry their own text colour; figure inks and underline marks lighten in the dark theme. A locked palette: no gradients, no transparency washes, no shadows; depth only from overlapping flat planes (the white paper sheet on a field). One grotesk, Archivo, in three widths: expanded for display, normal for text, condensed for labels. Square corners; 2px ink rules open a block and 1px rules divide rows; lists share a key column and a body column (the CV adds a heading column); publications numbered in a margin column.

STORY: The visitor reads the author's own headline, watches a multi-agent system work and can rearrange or extend it, meets the person under the line, then reaches papers, CV or email in one step.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav with the `ms22` wordmark and four links, each with a square in its page ink. The headline in expanded semibold at up to 88px, left, four lines. A full-width 2px ink line (the shore) at about two thirds of the height; on it, right, a multi-agent architecture about 475px wide with no text in it (user steer, 2026-11-15: bring back the little robots, add varied shapes, read at once as a sophisticated architecture): a large wine robot dome on the shore (the orchestrator), an ink router, five robots above it in pine, cobalt, sun and wine, and the things they use (a memory hexagon, store bars, document sheets, four outlined tools, a task queue and an exit on the shore), joined by 2px ink lines; a trace readout sits under the line. Portrait and bio hang under the shore at the left; name, role, affiliations and the Publications, Email and Download CV actions sit under the figure, inside the first viewport.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: The figure is a live simulation. A task dot rides in along the shore, the orchestrator fans messages out through the router, the robots look at and talk to each other and use memory, store, documents and tools, results return and the answer leaves along the shore; every hop is written to the trace readout. The visitor drags any shape and the lines follow, and a press on empty space adds a robot of their own (up to three; a fourth moves the oldest), which is wired in and used at once. MOTION GRAMMAR: one hero entrance (the headline rises word by word, the shore draws, the orchestrator rises from the shore and the agents arrive); hover feedback on every link and press feedback on every button and toggle; cross-page view transitions; nothing fades in on scroll.
